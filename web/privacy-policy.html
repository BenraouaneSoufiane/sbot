Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 6, 2026

This policy explains how sbot handles information when you use our website, reconciliation service, and WhatsApp integration. We use information to provide and secure the service; we do not sell personal information.

1. Who we are

sbot ("sbot," "we," "us," or "our") provides tools that help users configure, run, and monitor record-reconciliation checks. This policy applies to the sbot service at sbot.rest, including interactions with the sbot WhatsApp account.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may process:

  • WhatsApp information: your phone number or WhatsApp identifier, message identifier, message text, button or list selections, and our replies. We currently process supported text-based messages and do not intentionally process message attachments through the integration.
  • Workspace and reconciliation information: check names, instructions, schedules, data-source configurations, records submitted for reconciliation, results, exceptions, run history, and notification settings.
  • Connection information: destination addresses and credentials or tokens that you choose to provide for third-party data sources and notification channels.
  • Technical information: IP address, request time, browser or device information, webhook delivery data, and security or diagnostic logs generated when you access the service.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide the features you request; authenticate and route WhatsApp messages; understand and carry out supported reconciliation commands; run checks and deliver alerts; maintain conversation context; prevent duplicate webhook processing; troubleshoot, secure, and improve the service; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, your consent where requested, and compliance with law.

4. WhatsApp and automated processing

When you message sbot on WhatsApp, Meta sends your supported message content and identifiers to our webhook. sbot may use an automated language-processing service to classify your request into a limited set of actions, such as creating, editing, running, or stopping a check, and then sends a reply through the WhatsApp Cloud API. sbot restricts this interface from accessing credentials or general filesystem and command-execution tools.

Your use of WhatsApp is also governed by Meta's and WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policies. Their processing is independent of this policy.

5. How we share information

We may disclose information only as needed to:

  • Meta and WhatsApp to receive messages and deliver replies;
  • hosting, infrastructure, security, and automated-processing providers that operate the service on our behalf;
  • data-source and notification providers you configure, in order to retrieve records or send alerts;
  • professional advisers, authorities, or other parties when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or investigate abuse; and
  • a successor in connection with a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale of the service, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use WhatsApp message data for third-party advertising.

6. Retention

We retain workspace data, message and run history, settings, and webhook-processing records for as long as needed to provide and secure the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Short-term WhatsApp conversation context may be limited to recent exchanges. Retention can vary depending on workspace configuration, backup cycles, and legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including verification of signed WhatsApp webhook requests and access restrictions. No internet service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Do not send passwords, access tokens, private keys, financial account details, health information, or other highly sensitive information through WhatsApp.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may stop WhatsApp communications at any time by not messaging the account or by blocking it in WhatsApp. You may also delete your WhatsApp conversation from your device, but doing so does not automatically delete records already received by sbot.

To request deletion of sbot-held WhatsApp data or exercise another privacy right, email privacy@sbot.rest with the phone number used to contact the service and the request you want us to fulfill. We may ask you to verify your identity. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use contractual or other safeguards intended to protect information transferred internationally.

10. Children's privacy

sbot is a business service and is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the service. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information to us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. Material changes may also be communicated through the service where appropriate.

12. Contact us

For questions, complaints, or privacy requests, contact sbot at privacy@sbot.rest.
